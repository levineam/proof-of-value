# Product and research

**Maturity: proposed.** The first feed, invited cohort, contribution policy, and success measures are not yet operating or settled.

## Goal and boundary

Turn the self-referential Swarm feed into a bounded learning plan: an invited cohort discusses, builds, tests, documents, and critiques Proof of Value. Define a reviewable contribution rubric and a fixed evaluation window without claiming that a founder-led cohort proves stake-weighted judgment at scale.

## Prerequisites and owned areas

- Depend on the product brief, fixture-first shell, operations readiness gate, and visible provenance. Own proposed cohort brief, contribution criteria, research protocol, interview/survey prompts, metric definitions, and product copy that accurately labels maturity.
- Upstream contracts: **R1-R6, R14-R19; F1-F5; AE1, AE3, AE6-AE8; KTD7-KTD11.**

## Non-goals and open questions

Do not recruit a public beta, set token value/economics, turn research data into an investment claim, or decide unresolved voting and moderation policy in code. Open questions include who joins first; whether an AT post can nominate off-platform work; final eligible contribution types; downvote meaning; period/budget/curve; and privacy/retention rules.

## Acceptance examples

- The cohort brief says who may author, nominate, evaluate, and appeal; it distinguishes contribution criteria from settlement rules still open.
- A contribution rubric covers research, critique, rebuttal, code, design, documentation, experiment, and evidence without auto-admitting every post.
- The research plan names a fixed window and success signals such as cohort activation, qualified contributions, completed evaluations, reproducible provenance, participant understanding, and reported safety issues; it also records failure/stale signals and what evidence would revise the hypothesis.
